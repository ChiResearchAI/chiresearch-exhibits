Three applications, one platform

A working demonstration for Atlanta Regional Commission RFP 828 · ChiResearch Technologies, LLC · 7 November 2026

What this is. A working demonstration, not deployed production software and not an ARC product. All three applications below are configurations of a single platform with one shared engine, one data model and one administrative path — which is the architecture the proposal describes, shown rather than asserted. Everything runs on ARC's own published ArcGIS Online service (org opendata@arc): 45 indicators drawn from four ACS 2023 services across 159 counties, 538 municipalities and 2,796 census tracts. Indicator names are read from ARC's own field aliases, not retyped. No data is modelled, imputed or simulated.
