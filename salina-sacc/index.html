Common Operating Picture — role-based demonstration

Every figure on this page expands to its definition, its source and its exclusions. That is the point of the exhibit: a Common Operating Picture is not a screen, it is an agreement about what each number means, and the agreement has to be visible to the person reading it.

The executive view answers three questions and stops: does the airfield meet the standard it is held to, is weather exposure changing, and is any number on this page one the Authority could not defend if asked.

Primary runway wind coverage

RWY 17/35, all-weather, 10.5-kt allowable crosswind

94.74%
Against the FAA's 95% wind coverage recommendation.
0.26 points under the standard
Definition & source
Definition
Share of hourly observations in which the crosswind component on RWY 17/35 does not exceed 10.5 knots. Crosswind = wind speed × |sin(wind direction − runway true heading)|.
Standard
FAA AC 150/5300-13B Change 1, Airport Design (active, issued 16 Aug 2024; supersedes 13A, cancelled 31 Mar 2022). 10.5 kt is the allowable crosswind for Runway Design Code A-I and B-I.
Source
Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS archive, station SLN, hourly routine observations, 1 Jan 2016 – 31 Dec 2025 UTC.
Population
87,328 records retrieved; 85,824 used; 1,504 excluded for missing wind direction or speed. Calm observations (6,185, 7.21%) are counted as covered on every runway.
Known limits
Runway true headings are derived from the FAA magnetic designators plus the published 7°E variation, not from surveyed bearings. A production implementation would source runway geometry from the Authority's electronic ALP. See the governance card below.

Definition & source
Definition
An observation is below VFR when ceiling is under 3,000 ft or visibility is 5 statute miles or less. Ceiling is the lowest BKN, OVC or VV layer.
Why this is a governance example
Counting by ceiling alone gives 9,587 hours. Counting by visibility alone gives 2,345. Counting by either gives 11,331. All three are defensible; they differ by more than 4×. Until one is certified, "how much bad weather did we have" has no single answer.
Source
Same archive and period as above.
Known limits
Hourly routine observations only; specials (SPECI) are excluded, so short-lived deteriorations between the hour are not represented.

Definition & source
Definition
Below-VFR observations in each calendar month across all ten years, divided by all observations in that month — computed on the same 85,824-observation population used everywhere else on this page, so the denominators agree.
Why it is reported as a ratio
Because the absolute rates are easy to dismiss and the ratio is not. A single annual average conceals a sixfold swing, which is the kind of thing a dashboard exists to stop concealing.

One governance finding, shown rather than asserted

What a single unstated convention does to a reported number

METAR wind direction is true. FAA runway designators are magnetic. At SLN the difference is 7°. An integration that compares them directly — a quiet, ordinary mistake — changes this result:

RWY 17/35 wind coverage at 10.5 knots, corrected and uncorrected
RWY 17/35 at 10.5 ktCoverageAgainst 95%
Corrected for 7°E variation94.74%Under
Uncorrected95.29%Over

Same ten years, same 85,824 observations, same formula. Only the heading convention differs, and the verdict against an FAA standard reverses. Neither screen would look wrong.

Why this is in an executive view
The point
Because the failure is silent. A dashboard cannot tell leadership that a number is wrong; it can only tell them where the number came from. That is why runway geometry, magnetic variation and metric definitions belong in a governed semantic layer sourced from the Authority's ALP, and why establishing it is Phase 1 work rather than a configuration detail deferred to Phase 2.
Direction of the error
It is not uniform. The same uncorrected comparison moves RWY 12/30 the other way, from 84.20% to 81.64%. An error that moved every number the same way would be easier to notice.

The operations view is built around a single question an operations duty officer actually asks: which runway is usable, for which aircraft, and how often does that change.

The distribution is strongly bimodal north–south, which is what puts RWY 17/35 ahead of the other three and RWY 04/22 last.

Definition & source
Binning
Direction rounded to the nearest of 16 compass points (22.5° bins). Speed bands are 0–5, 6–10, 11–16, 17–21 and 22+ knots sustained. Calm observations carry no direction and are excluded from the petals, then reported separately.
Source
Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS archive, station SLN, 2016–2025, 85,824 observations with wind reported.

High-wind days

Any hour at or above 30 kt sustained

8.5/yr
85 days across ten years. Days on which lifts, signage, lighting and pavement-marking work are likely to be stood down.
Definition & source
Definition
A calendar day (UTC) containing at least one hourly observation with sustained wind of 30 knots or more. Gusts are not counted, because gust reporting is intermittent in this archive and including it would make the series inconsistent across years.
Why 30 knots
It is a working threshold chosen for this exhibit, not an Authority standard. In an engagement this is exactly the kind of number the Authority sets and the semantic layer then holds — once, for every report.

Caution
Below-VFR is a flight-operations measure, not a works measure. It is a reasonable proxy for "poor outdoor conditions" and nothing more. A works-specific index would combine wind, precipitation, temperature and daylight — and would have to be defined by the Authority before it could be reported.

What closes the airfield down

Binding element in 11,331 below-VFR hours

79.3%
Ceiling alone. Visibility alone accounted for 15.4%; both together for 5.3%.
Definition & source
Definition
For each below-VFR observation, whether the ceiling-derived category, the visibility-derived category, or both equally, produced the result.
Why ties are reported
601 hours had both at the same category. Assigning them to one element by convention would have overstated that element by about five points. They are shown separately instead.
